# YVR Water Fountains

The YVR water fountain enthusiast team drinks from public fountains around Vancouver, review them on Instagram at [@yvrwaterfountains](https://www.instagram.com/yvrwaterfountains/), and put the reviews on a [map](https://yvr-water-fountains.netlify.app/map).

## How a review works

Each visit gets one overall rating out of 10 and fun commentary. Subsequent visits to the same fountain is meant to be a new review with its own visit date, not an edit of the first one, so we can see whether a fountain gets better or worse over time. 

## The data 

Fountain locations come from two open data files kept in this repo: [City of Vancouver](data/raw/vancouver_fountains_raw.csv) (278 rows) and [City of Burnaby](data/raw/burnaby_fountains_raw.csv) (151 rows). We’ve also tracked down fountains that aren’t in any official database, so you’ll find water here that you won’t find anywhere else!
